# Refunds and Cancellation

This article covers Nimbus's refund policy, how to cancel your subscription, and what happens to your data after cancellation.

## Refund Policy

Nimbus offers a **14-day money-back guarantee** on your first purchase. If you are not satisfied with Nimbus within the first 14 days of your initial paid subscription, you can request a full refund.

**Conditions:**

- The 14-day window begins on the date of your first charge.
- The guarantee applies to the **first purchase only** — subsequent renewal charges and upgrades are not eligible for a refund under this policy.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer.

To request a refund within the 14-day window, contact our support team:

- **Pro plan:** Priority email support
- **Business plan:** Priority support
- **Enterprise plan:** Contact your dedicated customer success manager

## How to Cancel Your Subscription

Cancellation stops your subscription from auto-renewing at the next billing date. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period regardless of when you cancel.

1. Navigate to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Cancel subscription**.
3. Select a cancellation reason from the dropdown (optional but appreciated).
4. Click **Confirm cancellation**.

You will receive a confirmation email. Your workspace remains fully active until the billing period ends.

## What Happens After Cancellation

- Your account is **downgraded to the Free plan** at the end of the billing period.
- If your workspace has more than 3 members, you will be prompted to reduce the member count to 3 or fewer before the downgrade takes effect.
- Boards in excess of the Free plan's 2-board limit will be archived (not deleted). You can reactivate them by upgrading again.
- All your data — tasks, attachments, and history — is retained for **30 days** after the downgrade, after which excess data may be purged.

## Reactivating a Cancelled Subscription

You can reactivate your subscription at any time before or after the billing period ends:

1. Navigate to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Reactivate subscription** and choose your desired plan.
3. Confirm the charge. Upgrades are prorated from the reactivation date.

## Downgrading vs. Cancelling

- **Downgrading** (e.g., from Business to Pro) takes effect at the next renewal date. You are not charged the difference for the current period.
- **Cancelling** stops all future charges and reverts your workspace to the Free plan at period end.
