# Pricing and Billing

This article explains Nimbus plan pricing, billing cycles, payment processing, and how upgrades and downgrades work.

## Plans and Pricing

All prices are per member per month.

### Free

- **Price:** $0
- Up to 3 members
- 2 boards
- 100 automation runs per month
- Community support

The Free plan is ideal for individuals or small teams exploring Nimbus.

### Pro

- **$12 per member/month** billed annually
- **$14 per member/month** billed monthly
- Unlimited members and boards
- Timeline view
- 1,000 automation runs per month
- 250 MB file uploads per file
- All integrations (Slack, GitHub, GitLab, Google Drive, Figma, Zapier, webhooks)
- Priority email support

Annual billing saves approximately 15% compared to monthly billing.

### Business

- **$22 per member/month** billed annually
- Unlimited members and boards
- 10,000 automation runs per month
- 5 GB file uploads per file
- Reporting dashboards
- Google and Microsoft SSO
- Guest access (Owner/Admin can invite Guests)
- Priority support

Business is only available as an annual subscription.

### Enterprise

- **Custom pricing** — contact our sales team
- Everything in Business, plus:
  - SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning
  - Audit logs
  - Data residency (US or EU)
  - Dedicated customer success manager
  - 99.9% uptime SLA

## Billing Cycles

Nimbus supports two billing cycles:

- **Monthly** — billed each month on your subscription anniversary date
- **Annual** — billed once per year; saves approximately 15% versus monthly

You can switch from monthly to annual at any time. Your new annual rate takes effect at the next renewal date.

## Payment Processing

All payments are processed securely via **Stripe**. Nimbus accepts major credit and debit cards. Invoices are available in **Settings → Billing** after each charge.

## Upgrades and Downgrades

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately and are **prorated** — you are charged only for the remaining days in the current billing period at the new plan rate.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current billing period. You retain access to your current plan's features until that date.

## Changing Member Count

Adding a new member mid-cycle generates a prorated charge for the remainder of the billing period. Removing a member reduces the seat count at the next renewal.

## Viewing and Updating Billing Details

1. Navigate to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Review your current plan, next renewal date, and payment method.
3. Click **Update payment method** to change your card.
4. Click **Download invoice** next to any past charge to save a PDF receipt.

## Common Questions

**Can I pay with a purchase order or bank transfer?**
Yes, for Enterprise accounts. Contact our sales team to arrange invoiced billing.

**What happens if my payment fails?**
Nimbus will retry the charge over three days. If payment is not collected, your account is downgraded to the Free plan. Your data is retained for 30 days, giving you time to update your payment details.
