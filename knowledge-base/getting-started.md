# Getting Started with Nimbus

Welcome to Nimbus — your cloud-based project-management workspace. This guide walks you through creating your account, setting up your first board, and inviting your team so you can start collaborating right away.

## Create Your Account

1. Go to [app.nimbus.io](https://app.nimbus.io) and click **Sign up free**.
2. Enter your work email address and choose a password, or continue with Google or Microsoft.
3. Verify your email address using the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
4. Enter your name and your organization name to complete your profile.

## Choose a Plan

Nimbus offers four plans to fit teams of every size:

- **Free** — Up to 3 members, 2 boards, and 100 automation runs per month. Community support included. Great for small teams trying Nimbus for the first time.
- **Pro** — $12 per member/month billed annually (or $14 billed monthly). Unlimited boards, timeline view, 1,000 automation runs/month, 250 MB file uploads, all integrations, and priority email support.
- **Business** — $22 per member/month billed annually. Everything in Pro, plus 10,000 automation runs/month, 5 GB file uploads, reporting dashboards, Google & Microsoft SSO, and guest access.
- **Enterprise** — Custom pricing. Adds SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning, audit logs, data residency (US or EU), a dedicated customer success manager, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

You can start with the Free plan and upgrade at any time. Upgrades are prorated.

## Create Your First Board

1. From your workspace home, click **+ New board**.
2. Choose a template (Kanban, Sprint, or Blank) or start from scratch.
3. Give your board a name and an optional description.
4. Click **Create board** to open the board editor.

## Add Columns and Tasks

1. Click **+ Add column** to create your first workflow stage (e.g., To Do, In Progress, Done).
2. Click **+ Add task** inside a column to create a task.
3. Enter a task title, assign a team member, and set a due date.
4. Click into the task card to add a description, attachments, or subtasks.

## Invite Team Members

1. Navigate to **Settings → Members**.
2. Click **Invite members** and enter your teammates' email addresses.
3. Select a role for each invitee: **Owner**, **Admin**, **Member**, or **Guest** (Guest access requires Business or Enterprise).
4. Click **Send invites**. Invitees will receive an email with a link to join your workspace.

On the Free plan, your workspace is limited to 3 members total.

## Next Steps

- Explore the **Timeline view** (Pro and above) to visualize project schedules.
- Set up **Integrations** with Slack, GitHub, Google Drive, and more via **Settings → Integrations**.
- Configure **Automation** rules to reduce repetitive work.
- Visit the Help Center or community forum if you have questions.
