# Nimbus — Canonical Fact Sheet

This document is the single source of truth for all Nimbus product facts. Every help-center article must match these details exactly.

---

## Product

**Nimbus** is a cloud-based project-management tool offering boards, timeline view, workflow automation, and reporting dashboards.

---

## Plans & Pricing

Prices are per member per month.

| Plan       | Price                              | Members       | Boards     | Automation Runs/mo | File Uploads   | Support           | Notable Features                                                 |
|------------|------------------------------------|---------------|------------|--------------------|----------------|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Free       | $0                                 | Up to 3       | 2 boards   | 100                | —              | Community         | Core boards, basic task management                               |
| Pro        | $12 billed annually / $14 monthly  | Unlimited     | Unlimited  | 1,000              | 250 MB         | Priority email    | Timeline view, all integrations, 250 MB file uploads             |
| Business   | $22 billed annually                | Unlimited     | Unlimited  | 10,000             | 5 GB           | Priority          | Reporting dashboards, Google & Microsoft SSO, guest access       |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing                     | Unlimited     | Unlimited  | Advanced/custom    | Custom         | Dedicated CSM     | SAML SSO + SCIM provisioning, audit logs, data residency (US/EU), 99.9% uptime SLA |

**Annual billing saves approximately 15%** compared to monthly billing.

---

## Billing

- **Payment processor:** Stripe
- **Billing cycles:** Monthly or annual
- **Upgrades:** Prorated immediately
- **Annual discount:** ~15% savings over monthly

---

## Refunds & Cancellation

- **Money-back guarantee:** 14-day refund on first purchase only
- **Cancellation:** Stops auto-renewal; access continues until the end of the current billing period
- No refunds after the 14-day window on subsequent charges

---

## Roles

| Role   | Description                                         |
|--------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Owner  | Full account control, billing management            |
| Admin  | Manage members, boards, and workspace settings      |
| Member | Standard access to boards and tasks                 |
| Guest  | Limited access; available on Business and Enterprise|

---

## Integrations

Connect via **Settings → Integrations** using OAuth.

- Slack
- GitHub
- GitLab
- Google Drive
- Figma
- Zapier
- Outgoing webhooks

All integrations are available on Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans.
